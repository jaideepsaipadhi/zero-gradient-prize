\section*{Statement on the use of generative AI}
%% TBD: if authorship becomes joint, rephrase in the plural and add a CRediT statement.
The author used Claude (Anthropic; model Claude Opus~5.5) extensively in this work, as a research and writing assistant. Specifically, it was used for:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{developing proofs}: proposing proof strategies and intermediate lemmas, and working out calculations, which the author then checked and revised;
\item \emph{checking proofs}: separate sessions of the model were instructed to act as critical referees of individual arguments; their objections led to corrections, which are recorded in the repository;
\item \emph{code}: writing and running the three-dimensional finite element solver of Section~\ref{sec:numerics} and the scripts that produce the exact-arithmetic and interval-arithmetic certificates;
\item \emph{literature search}: locating and summarising references; where a source was read only through such a summary or a text-extraction tool rather than in the original, this is stated in Section~\ref{sec:open};
\item \emph{drafting and editing} the manuscript, including this revision for readability.
\end{itemize}
We did not treat the model's output as verified. Every computer-assisted statement is backed by a certificate in exact rational or interval arithmetic that can be rerun from the published code (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}), several of them independently re-derived; the proofs were checked by independent referee passes as described above, and the author reviewed the resulting corrections; and all numerical results are reproducible from the listed commands and logs. The author directed the project, made the mathematical and editorial decisions, and takes full responsibility for the content, including any errors. The AI system is not an author of this work.
