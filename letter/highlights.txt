Answers Scott's prize question on weak no-slip at polygons inscribed in curved walls
The printed Nitsche form lacks a pressure traction, so flow leaks through the wall
The leak is the leading error, with constant 1 in the slip and energy norms
A mean-free traction gives the sharp H1 error h_Γ^(3/2) + h^k for general Stokes data
Nitsche penalty ~ h/min|e| suffices, and is necessary for well-shaped boundary cells
