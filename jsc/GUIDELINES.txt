1. Manuscript format
   [G] "Manuscripts should be submitted in LaTeX. Please use Springer's LaTeX macro package and choose the
       formatting option 'smallextended'."  ('smallextended' is an option of the old svjour3 class; the zip
       the page actually links, 468198_LaTeX_DL_..._240419, is the Springer Nature sn-jnl template package.
       The page text is out of date relative to its own link. We use sn-jnl.)
   [G] "For first (initial) submissions, we require a single file containing your manuscript as a minimum
       (Word or PDF)."  "For revised submissions we strongly recommend to upload the editable source files."
       -> Any reasonable format is accepted at first submission (a PDF suffices); source files are needed at
       revision. [G] does not use the phrase "Your Paper Your Way", but the effect is the same.
   Template sample file: "Please do not use \input{...} to include other tex files. Submit your LaTeX
       manuscript as one .tex document." -> jsc/main.tex is a single flattened file (generated by flatten.py).
   Template obtained: sn-jnl.cls and sn-mathphys-num.bst from the GitHub mirror rstudio/rticles
       (inst/rmarkdown/templates/springer/skeleton, commit 2a0ee07, 2026-09-16; its template.tex is headed
       "Version 2.1 April 2023"). kpsewhich sn-jnl.cls: not installed; not on CTAN/tlmgr. If Springer's own
       zip [T] is newer, swapping in its sn-jnl.cls should need no source changes (same user commands).

2. Title page [G]: concise title; author names; affiliations (institution, department, city, state,
   country); corresponding author clearly indicated with an active e-mail; 16-digit ORCID "if available".
   Author contributions "should be listed at the separate title page" (recommended, free text or CRediT).

3. Abstract [G]: "Please provide an abstract of 150 to 250 words."  -> jsc abstract: 243 words (the
   paper2d abstract had ~410 words and was shortened; no new claims).
4. Keywords [G]: "Please provide 4 to 6 keywords which can be used for indexing purposes." -> 6 used.
5. Classification [G]: MSC codes required (www.ams.org/msc). -> 65N30 (primary), 65N12, 65N15, 76D07.

6. Declarations [G]: competing interests / funding "should be summarized in a statement and placed in a
   'Declarations' section before the reference list under a heading of 'Funding' and/or 'Competing
   interests'."  Data availability statement mandatory (Springer Nature research data policy); code
   availability strongly encouraged ("Reproducibility": "strongly encourages authors to make available all
   computer codes and/or data" via GitHub, Bitbucket or similar). Ethics approval / consent: only for
   human/animal research (here: Not applicable). The template's Declarations list (Funding, Competing
   interests, Ethics approval and consent to participate, Consent for publication, Data availability,
   Materials availability, Code availability, Author contributions) is used in jsc/main.tex.
   Competing interests: financial and non-financial interests within 3 years of beginning the work.
   -> The Scott prize (PPL 115) is disclosed as in paper2d.

7. Generative AI / LLMs
   [G] "Large Language Models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, do not currently satisfy our authorship criteria.
       Notably an attribution of authorship carries with it accountability for the work, which cannot be
       effectively applied to LLMs. Use of an LLM should be properly documented in the Methods section (and
       if a Methods section is not available, in a suitable alternative part) of the manuscript. The use of
       an LLM (or other AI-tool) for "AI assisted copy editing" purposes does not need to be declared. [...]
       These AI-assisted improvements may include wording and formatting changes to the texts, but do not
       include generative editorial work and autonomous content creation. In all cases, there must be human
       accountability for the final version of the text [...]"
   [P] Springer's traffic-light AI policy: Red (not permitted) includes "Generating hypotheses, analyses or
       conclusions and presenting them as human-derived", "Using an LLM to generate core research reasoning
       without disclosure", "Assigning authorship or accountability to AI systems or tools", "Fabricating
       data, citations or results". Amber (with oversight and disclosure): suggesting approaches, drafting,
       comparing with the literature, extensive writing support. "Accountability for scholarly content [...]
       cannot be delegated to AI systems."
   -> Our use (proofs, code, literature, drafting) is far beyond copy editing and MUST be disclosed; it is
      permitted only because it is disclosed. jsc/main.tex puts the full AI statement (wording unchanged
      from paper2d) in the methods section, Section 3.8 "Use of generative AI" (end of Section 3 "Setting,
      methods and tools", so no existing number changes), with a pointer in Declarations and in Author
      contributions; the AI is not an author. [G] also requires confirming compliance with the generative-AI
      *images* policy at submission: our figures are plotted from computed data by scripts, not AI images.

8. References [G]: "Reference citations in the text should be identified by numbers in square brackets."
   Numbered list, DOIs as https://doi.org/..., journal abbreviations per ISSN LTWA. -> sn-mathphys-num
   (numbered; the Springer "Math and Physical Sciences" style).
9. Page limits / article types: none stated in [G]. (jsc/main.pdf: 64 pages in sn-jnl single column.)
10. Reviewers [G]: "Authors are welcome to suggest suitable reviewers and/or request the exclusion of
   certain individuals." Suggestions should come with institutional e-mail addresses or verification links.
11. Cover letter: not mentioned in [G]; Editorial Manager has a cover-letter field (standard).
12. Preprints: not addressed in [G]; Springer Nature's general policy permits preprints (e.g. Zenodo,
   arXiv) -- disclose the Zenodo extended version in the cover letter (done).
13. Other [G]: COPE compliance, plagiarism screening (iThenticate -- the Zenodo extended version and the
   AML letter will match; this is declared in the cover letter), permissions for reused figures (none;
   all figures are ours), publishing model hybrid (Open Choice optional, not required).
