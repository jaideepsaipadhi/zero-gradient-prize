Method: ISOPARAMETRIC curved tetrahedral mesh fitting Omega (Lenoir/[20] maps), Scott-Vogelius P_k/P_{k-1}, k>=3,
on Alfeld splits, velocity space via Piola transform from reference element (H(div)-conforming, not H^1-conforming
near boundary); bilinear form with interior-face consistency terms {d_n u}.[[v]] (no penalty); Dirichlet BC imposed
strongly on the curved boundary (V_h subset H_0(div)). Thm 4.4: ||u-u_h||_{1,h} <~ h^{l-1}||u|| + nu^{-1}|f-f_h|_{Z_h*}.
Pressure robustness (Sec 5): commuting operators Pi^W_h, Pi^Sigma_h on curved meshes (pullback + Fu-Guzman-Neilan
reference commuting interpolant + covariant pushforward), f_h = Pi^W_h f; Cor 5.2: ||u-u_h||_{1,h} <~ h^k ||u||_{H^{k+2}}
independent of p and nu. 2D predecessor: Neilan-Otus SINUM 2021 (Cor 6.2).

Overlap with our work: NONE on (a) Nitsche/weak BCs, (b) inscribed polygon/polyhedron (straight-sided) geometry,
(c) non-robustness of the Gjerde-Scott printed method / leak law, (d) our CNS*-R local BDM boundary load correction
(their device is a global commuting-interpolant load, different construction and setting; related in spirit -> cite
alongside Neilan-Otus as prior art for load reconstruction on curved domains), (e) h_Gamma^{3/2} rates/sharpness.
Positioning consequence: isoparametric Piola SV attains optimal h^k in 3D; our paper must present the inscribed-
polyhedron + Nitsche route as the low-cost straight-sided alternative whose best rate is h_Gamma^{3/2}+h^k (sharp),
not as competing on rate.
