Companion to paper/sections/12_three_d.tex.  Nothing in paper/ or letter/ was edited.  Nothing committed.

CODE (code3d/)
  svn3d.py      solver.  Continuous P_k velocity (k>=3; k=3,4 run) with discontinuous full-P_{k-1}
                pressure on every micro-tet of an ALFELD split mesh.  Forms copied from code/svn_k.py:
                a_h = 1/2(Du,Dv); Nitsche -<d_n u,v> -<u,d_n v> +(mu/h)<u,v> on Gamma_h, h = max tet diam,
                n points INTO P_h; GS = no pressure traction, CNS* = +<p - pbar_Gamma(p), v.n>.
                Vectorised numpy assembly (exact reference integrals + affine maps).  Pressure eliminated by
                the iterated-penalty method (r = 1e4; K = A + r B^T M^-1 B factorised once with MKL PARDISO
                LDL^T; the CNS* traction enters only the right-hand side; identical fixed point).  Stops when
                ||div u_h|| < 1e-13 and momentum residual < 1e-11 (or round-off stagnation).  Cross-checked
                against the nonsymmetric-LU/Sherman-Morrison variant (symK=False): same u_h to 1e-12.
                Also: local_threshold() = per-boundary-micro-tet SUFFICIENT coercivity threshold on V_h.
  exact3d.py    exact solutions (sympy): mms (box), rot = (1-r^-3) w x x, p=0 (u=0, p const on |x|=1);
                sph = classical Stokes flow past the fixed unit sphere, U=(1,.4,-.3), p = -(3/2)U.x/r^3;
                P = pure pressure (u=0, f=grad p, same p) -> GS output is exactly the traction response.
  run3d.py      drivers (box / sph / interp);  table3d.py tables;  penalty3d.py Z_h coercivity threshold
                (PARDISO inertia; validated vs dense eigvalsh, penalty_dense_check.log);  infsup3d.py discrete
                inf-sup (dense, coarse only);  patch_test.py;  local_*.sh = exactly what was run here;
                run_pod3d.sh = finer levels (commands + memory estimates below).
  Data: notes/v6/num3d/results.jsonl (one record per solve), *.log, tables_local.txt (full tables).

MESH.  Equiangular N x N grid on each face of the box (-L,L)^3, L=2, radially projected to the unit sphere:
  the projected points are the vertices of P_h (quad diagonals chosen so P_h is CONVEX), 12N^2 triangular
  faces; layers r_j = |b|^(j/m), m = N/2, prisms split into 3 tets by the min-index rule (conforming), then
  Alfeld split (x4).  Every boundary face is a whole face of P_h or lies on the box (asserted).  Shape
  regularity uniform (min vol/diam^3 = 0.0074-0.0079 for N=2..24), but h/hG drifts 3.26, 4.14, 4.79, 5.24
  (N=2,4,6,8) toward ~7: the family is not exactly self-similar at small N.

 (1) patch test (patch_test.log): P_k solution reproduced to 1e-9..1e-11 (H1) for k=3,4, Nitsche and strong BC.
 (2) box (-1,1)^3, smooth manufactured solution, consistent Nitsche ((Du)n and pressure traction) on all faces,
     mu=400 (> local sufficient threshold 208 (k=3), 333 (k=4)):
       k=3  N=2..6 (to 79k vel dofs): H1 rates 2.78 2.86 2.90 2.92 -> 3;  L2 3.78 3.98 4.04 4.06 -> 4

       ||div u_h||_L2 = 1e-13 .. 1.5e-12 in every run (exactly divergence-free up to round-off).
 (3) inf-sup (infsup3d, V_0 = zero on whole boundary, A = vector Laplacian): exactly ONE zero eigenvalue
     (constants) in every case, no spurious modes; beta_h on L^2_0 = 0.142, 0.149, 0.150 (box N=1,2,3)
     and 0.069 (sphere mesh N=2).  Consistent with (IS3) for Alfeld k=3; uniformity only seen on the box.
 (4) coercivity: Z_h threshold muZ* (r-independent, r=1e5..1e7): k=3: 76.3 (N=2), 148.0 (N=4);
     k=4: 193.6 (N=2).  muZ* grows with rho ~ h/hG, as mu_0 = 4 kappa rho C_I^2 predicts.  Local sufficient
     V_h thresholds 216/341/421/476 (N=2/4/6/8).  All sphere runs use mu = 400 > muZ* (also 200 and 1000 for P).

INTERPRETATION / HONESTY
 * CONFIRMED at these levels: exact divergence-freeness; optimal h^k on the box (k=3,4); stable pair with no
   spurious pressure modes; the GS leak law in 3D with universal constant -> 1 in slip AND energy
   (P test, three mu values; D = GS - CNS* in the sph test: slip 0.98, leak 0.93); CNS* removes the leak
   (leak correlation -0.38 -> -0.06).
 * NOT YET CONFIRMED: the asymptotic RATES h^{3/2} (both methods, rot) and GS rate 1 / CNS* rate 3/2 (sph) in
   H1.  Only two levels fit locally, and they are PRE-ASYMPTOTIC: observed hG-rates 2.4-2.5 are dominated by
   the P_3 approximation error (nodal-interpolation H1 error 1.63 -> 0.19, even LARGER than the FE error at
   N=2) and by the O(hG^2) trace mismatch ||u~||_Gh (|e|_Gh rate 2.4-2.5, expected 2).  At N=4 the FE error
   is already 1.7-2.2x the interpolation error, i.e. the geometric (hG^{3/2}) part is taking over; its rate
   needs N >= 8.  In the sph test the GS leak (D, 0.064) is still small next to the geometric error (0.33),
   so GS rate 1 vs CNS* rate 3/2 in total H1 will only separate at much finer levels (ratio ~ hG^{1/2}/mu).
 * The drift of h/hG (3.3 -> 5.2) also contaminates rates in hG; rates in h are listed as r(h).
