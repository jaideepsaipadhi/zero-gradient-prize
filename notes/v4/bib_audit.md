# Bibliography audit (v4), 2026-09-28

This audit covers `paper/refs.bib` on branch `v3-draft` and the `\cite[...]` locators in `paper/sections/*.tex`. The corrected copy is `notes/v4/refs_verified.bib`. `paper/refs.bib` was not edited. The corrected copy passes `bibtex` with `amsplain`; the only warning is the expected missing pages for Eickmann et al.

**Access limits.** The bash sandbox could not reach any external host: Crossref, arXiv, doi.org, AMS and Zenodo all returned a 403 from the egress proxy. Every check below was therefore done through the web-fetch and web-search tools.

- Crossref was rate-limited (HTTP 429) after four lookups. The records for `10.1090/mcom/3346` and `10.1137/22M1533943` were not read there; other sources were used for both.
- ams.org article pages returned 403, apart from the issue table of contents for vol. 84(295) and one PDF (Zhang 2011).
- zenodo.org, aimsciences.org (the published ACSE version of Gjerde–Scott), epubs.siam.org and Google Books are blocked by robots.txt.

## 3. Citations that claim something the source does not say

1. **Intro l.5: the h^{3/2} observation is credited to GjerdeScott2024.** Their paper (arXiv) reports rates 2.06, 1.12 and 1.83 and no h^{3/2} statement. The h^{3/2} sentence is in Scott's prize PDF. Cite `ScottPrize`.
2. **§12 l.34: "a robustness caveat at nearly singular vertices, as tabulated in [FMS, Table 2.1]".** The WF row of Table 2.1 has no caveat and cites Fabien–Guzmán–Neilan–Zytoon, not GLN 2022. The caveat belongs to the 2D k ≥ 4 row.
3. **§12 l.36: the Neilan 2015 description.** According to FMS, Neilan's conditional (conjectured mesh condition) k ≥ 6 result, Prop. 6.5, is an extension of Zhang's Scott–Vogelius result. It is not a property of the extra-continuity pairs. This needs checking against Neilan's paper; the disclaimer "we do not claim that this settles (IS3)" may be too dismissive of a conditional SV result.
4. **Galdi locators.** "Thm. III.3.1" for the explicit star-shaped constant may be Lemma III.3.1 in the 2011 edition. "Lemma III.3.4" is unconfirmed, and an Exercise III.3.4 exists. None of the four Galdi numbers could be confirmed.
5. **Minor points:**
   - "For general Stokes data" is not in the prize wording.
   - Definition GS "as printed" flips the sign of b(·,·) and reads V_h as not vanishing on Γ_h, while the printed text says it does.
   - The quotation marks around "k ≥ d for Alfeld splits" mark a paraphrase.
   - The bib's howpublished "Ridgway Scott Foundation" has no source.
